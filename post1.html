Step 1: Finding Profitable Domains

Your success starts with picking the right domains.

Where to Look:

  • Namecheap (cheap and beginner-friendly)
  • GoDaddy Auctions (high activity)
  • ExpiredDomains.net (best for hidden opportunities)
  • Flippa (domains + websites)

What to Look For:

  • Short (under 15 characters)
  • Easy to spell and remember
  • Keyword-focused (AI, crypto, health, finance, etc.)
  • Sounds like a real brand
Good Example: FastAItools.com Bad Example: xqz-124-site.net

Goal: Buy domains for $5–$15 that could sell for $100+.

Step 2: Buying Domains the Smart Way

  • Start with 1–3 domains only
  • Don’t overspend early
  • Avoid trademarked names

Track everything in a simple spreadsheet:

  • Domain Name
  • Purchase Price
  • Idea for use
  • Target resale price

This turns it into a system instead of guessing.

Step 3: Add Value (Where Most People Mess Up)

Instead of just owning a domain, make it look useful and real.

Do This:

  • Create a simple landing page
  • Add 1–3 pieces of content
  • Show how it could be monetized

Tools:

  • WordPress
  • Wix / Squarespace
  • GitHub Pages
A blank domain might sell for $20 The same domain with a simple site could sell for $150+

Step 4: Where to Sell Your Domains

  • Sedo
  • Flippa
  • GoDaddy Auctions
  • Afternic

Listing Tip:

  • Explain who would use the domain
  • Highlight niche and keywords
“Perfect domain for an AI startup or SaaS tool. Short, brandable, and keyword-rich.”

Step 5: Get Traffic & Buyers

  • Post on Reddit (r/Entrepreneur, r/SideHustle)
  • Share on Twitter/X
  • Reach out to small businesses

Even a few targeted views can lead to a sale.
