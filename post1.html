Step 1: Finding Profitable Domains

Your success starts with picking the right domains.

Where to Look:

  • Namecheap (cheap and beginner-friendly)
  • GoDaddy Auctions (high activity)
  • ExpiredDomains.net (best for hidden opportunities)
  • Flippa (domains + websites)

What to Look For:

  • Short (under 15 characters)
  • Easy to spell and remember
  • Contains keywords (AI, crypto, health, finance, etc.)
  • Sounds like a real brand
Good Example: FastAItools.com Bad Example: xqz-124-site.net

Goal: Find domains you can buy for $5–$15 that someone else might want for $100+.

Step 2: Buying Domains the Smart Way

  • Start with 1–3 domains only
  • Don’t overspend early
  • Avoid trademarked names

Use a simple tracker (Google Sheets):

  • Domain Name
  • Purchase Price
  • Idea for use
  • Target resale price

This helps you treat domain flipping like a system—not guessing.

Step 3: Add Value (This Is Where Most People Fail)

Most beginners skip this step—and that’s why they don’t make money.

Instead of just owning a domain, you want to make it look useful and real.

3 Simple Ways to Do That:

  • Create a basic landing page (what the site could be)
  • Add 1–3 blog posts in the niche
  • Show how it could make money (ads, affiliate links, etc.)

Easy Tools:

  • WordPress
  • Wix / Squarespace
  • GitHub Pages (free option)
Example: A blank domain might sell for $20 The same domain with a simple site could sell for $150+

Step 4: Where to Sell Your Domains

  • Sedo
  • Flippa
  • GoDaddy Auctions
  • Afternic

How to List Properly:

  • Write a clear title
  • Explain who would use the domain
  • Mention niche and keywords
Example: “Perfect domain for an AI startup, blog, or SaaS tool. Short, brandable, and keyword-rich.”

Step 5: Get Traffic & Buyers

  • Post on Reddit (r/Entrepreneur, r/SideHustle)
  • Share on Twitter/X
  • Contact small businesses directly

Even 1–2 buyers seeing your domain can be enough to make a sale.
